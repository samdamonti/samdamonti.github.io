DOC - China: From West to East

For Northeastern's Summer II semester in 2024, I did a Chinese language month-long study abroad as part of Northeastern's Dialogue of Civilizations (DOC) program in Kunming, the capital of Yunnan Province, China. We studied Chinese at Yunnan Nationalities University 云南民族大学. We also stopped by Beijing, saw some incredible parts of the city, and climbed a really big wall.
